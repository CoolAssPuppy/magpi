## Description

Closed device, 1.2m onto concrete, landing on the hinge 2 edge. Four units, three cracked pane 3 at the corner nearest hinge 2. Pane 3 is an inner panel so a person cannot see the crack until they open the phone, which is worse.

Face drops, corner drops and back drops all pass at 1.2m.
