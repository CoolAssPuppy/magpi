## Description

Click a PDF on a thread and it downloads. To read a rate confirmation you get a
file in your downloads folder, open it in whatever your machine opens PDFs with,
read one number off it, and then have a copy of a customer document on your
laptop forever. Same for the delivery photos, which are the most looked at
attachments we have and which open as a download of a JPEG.

The ask is an inline viewer. PDF and image at minimum. Page through a multi page
bill of lading without leaving the thread.

Roughly: a viewer component in the thread pane, a signed short lived URL per
attachment, and a decision about whether we render PDFs client side or generate
page images at ingest. Client side is less infrastructure and worse on a phone.
Page images at ingest is more storage and better everywhere. Nobody has costed
either because nobody has been asked to.

Raised by three customers on the May advisory call and by Rosa on behalf of two
accounts. It has been on a list since 2024 under a different number.
