## Description

Rust is the oxide red frame finish. It passes every measurement on the finish durability page. Colour holds inside delta E 1.5 under the Meniscus-C coating, scratch passes, abrasion passes, light fastness passes.

It needs two anodising passes. A base anodise, then a second dyed pass. A single pass comes out uneven at the hinge knuckles where the section changes, and uneven at the knuckles is exactly where a person's thumb sits when the phone is shut.

That was known on 24 August and the plan was to book the second pass into the tooling window in the first week of September. The polymer reversal on 27 August means a die reorder, and the die reorder takes the window.

This issue is to find a second slot before the pilot line, or to say there is not one.
