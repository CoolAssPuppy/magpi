## Description

Opened the same afternoon we reversed the outer layer to Meniscus-C polymer.

The Suwon stack goes over the whole outer face and the rear cover, not only the active display area, so it sits on top of the colour layer on every finish. It is not optically neutral. Measured on a white tile it comes in at b* +1.9 against +0.2 for the Kyoto glass stack.

Five of the six finishes will not care. Tide is a pale blue at the lowest b* of the six by a distance, which means it has the least room before a warm cast changes the shade rather than shifting it.

Measure it rather than argue about it. Spectrophotometer, D65, 10 degree observer, five points per panel, against the master swatch. Gate is delta E 2000 under 1.5, which is the number a printer, a case supplier and a swatch card can all work from.
