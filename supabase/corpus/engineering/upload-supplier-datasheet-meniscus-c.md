1. DESCRIPTION

MC-62A is a multi-layer transparent polymer cover film for foldable display
applications. Construction is a polyimide base, an optically clear adhesive
layer, and a proprietary siloxane hard coat with an optional anti-fingerprint
top layer. The film is supplied in roll form, slit to customer width, with a
protective liner on both faces.

MC-62A revision 4 differs from revision 3 in the anti-fingerprint chemistry
only. The change was made in July 2026. Hard coat, base film and adhesive are
unchanged.

3. BEND PERFORMANCE

MC-62A is qualified to a minimum static bend radius of 2.8 mm and a minimum
dynamic bend radius of 3.0 mm. Below 3.0 mm dynamic, creasing is observed
before 100,000 folds and the film is outside its qualified envelope.

  Bend radius    Folds to visible crease    Test condition
  2.8 mm         96,000                     static hold, 72 h, then folded
  3.0 mm         184,000                    dynamic, 23 C
  3.2 mm         > 200,000, no crease       dynamic, 23 C
  4.0 mm         > 200,000, no crease       dynamic, 23 C
  5.0 mm         > 200,000, no crease       dynamic, 23 C

Folding is 0 to 180 degrees at 60 folds per minute, hard coat on the outside of
the bend, 23 C and 50 percent RH. Visible crease is defined by Suwon Advanced
Films internal procedure SAF-QM-118, gloss deviation greater than 3.0 GU at 60
degrees.

4. CUSTOMER NOTE ON HAZE

The 200,000 fold haze figure in section 2 is measured on the Suwon integrating
sphere against SAF-QM-121. Customers measuring on a different instrument should
expect an offset, commonly 0.1 to 0.2 percent higher on matte-coated films.
Suwon Advanced Films will correlate instruments on request.

5. HANDLING

  - Remove the liner immediately before lamination. Do not store unlinered.
  - Class 1000 or better during lamination. Particles under the film cause
    point defects that are visible at any angle.
  - Lamination temperature 55 to 70 C, dwell 90 seconds, pressure 0.35 MPa.
  - Do not exceed 80 C at any point. The hard coat yellows above 85 C.
  - Roll stock is to be conditioned at line ambient for a minimum of 8 hours
    before slitting.
