SAMPLE DESCRIPTION

Sixteen (16) outer face panels supplied by the client, four panels in each of
four finishes identified by the client as INK, CHALK, MOSS and EMBER. All
panels carry the client's production hard coat and anti-fingerprint stack,
vendor lot SW-2609-B.

client as withdrawn finishes. These were measured and reported but were not

METHOD

  Specular gloss         ASTM D523, 60 degree geometry
  Points per panel       5, three on the flat area and two spanning the
                         client's nominated fold line
  Instrument             Calibrated against certified black glass standard,
                         start and end of each session
  Colour                 CIE L*a*b*, D65 illuminant, 10 degree observer,
                         specular component included
  Reference              Client master swatch, one per finish, supplied
                         2026-09-02
  Colour difference      delta E 2000

  Observer panel         Three observers, uncorrected or corrected to normal
                         vision, 3000K diffuse panel at one metre, eye
                         distance 400 mm, panels presented in random order
                         and orientation. Observers were asked to report any
                         line, band or discontinuity without being told that
                         one might be present.

OBSERVATIONS

All four production finishes sit above their master swatch on flat gloss, by
between 0.2 and 1.2 GU. The direction is consistent across all four and is
attributed to the coating rather than to the colour layer.

EMBER is the highest gloss of the four production finishes and carries the
largest fold line deviation at 2.3 GU against a 2.5 GU gate. The client is
advised that a margin of 0.2 GU is within the reproducibility of this method
between laboratories, and that a finish this close to a gate should be
monitored per lot rather than qualified once.

The single observer finding on EMBER panel 3 is consistent with the gloss
result rather than independent of it, and both point at the same finish.

LIMITATIONS

Panels are not devices. This report measures flat panels carrying the
production coating and does not reproduce the assembled stack, the adhesive, the
frame or a fold line that has been cycled. Fold line locations were nominated by
the client and marked on the reverse of each panel.
