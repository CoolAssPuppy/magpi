SAMPLE DESCRIPTION

Twenty-four (24) coupons of ultra-thin glass, nominal thickness 30 micron,
supplied by the client and identified by the client as UTG-3, with hard coat
and anti-reflective stack applied. Coupons measured 120 mm x 40 mm. Coupons
were received in twelve individually sealed packs of two and were handled with
vacuum tooling only.

Client instruction was to determine cyclic bend life at two bend radii, 3.2 mm
and 4.0 mm, and to report the distribution rather than a single figure.

METHOD

Cyclic bend fatigue by the client's specified profile, in the absence of an
applicable standard for this material and geometry. Where a standard method was
available it was followed and is cited.

  Fold profile           0 to 180 degrees, hard coat on the outside of the bend
  Rate                   30 cycles per minute
  Environment            23 +/- 1 C, 45 +/- 5 percent RH
  Termination            visible fracture, or 300,000 cycles, whichever first
  Inspection             optical, 10x, at 10,000 cycle intervals
  Coating adhesion       ASTM D3359, cross hatch, before and after cycling
  Sample size            12 coupons per radius

OBSERVATIONS

At 3.2 mm the distribution is wide. A shape parameter of 3.1 indicates
variability consistent with flaw-initiated fracture in a brittle material,
where the governing quantity is the largest flaw present in the bend zone
rather than the mean strength of the material.

At 4.0 mm, nine of twelve coupons reached the 300,000 cycle termination without
fracture and no fit was attempted.

The client is advised that a design target expressed as a mean life is not
appropriate for a material with this distribution, and that a lower percentile,
such as B10, is the figure normally used for a component whose failure is
visible to a user.

LIMITATIONS

Coupons are not devices. This test does not reproduce the constraint of a
laminated stack, an adhesive layer, a hinge mechanism or a display underneath.
Results should be treated as a material ranking and not as a device life
prediction.
