## Description

We committed to IPX4, splash resistance from any direction, in the product spec. Three hinges is three times the number of places water gets in, and the hinge 2 gap opens to 0.9mm at half fold.

Current state: closed passes, fully open passes, half fold fails at hinge 2 within 90 seconds.
