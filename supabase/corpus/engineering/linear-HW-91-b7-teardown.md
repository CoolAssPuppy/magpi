## Description

Find out whether B7 failed because of the glass, the adhesive, or the hinge. If it is the hinge we have a bigger problem than a material choice.

**Sam Lindqvist** commented · 2026-08-31
> Teardown done. Cross sections under the microscope.
>
> Fracture starts in the hardcoat, not the glass core and not the OCA below it. Origin is a cluster of microfractures about 8mm from the hinge 2 centerline, which is exactly where the fold radius is tightest on the outboard side. The glass core cracked after, following the hardcoat.
>
> Hinge itself is fine. Torque on B7 hinge 2 was inside 6% at the point of failure, cam surfaces show normal polish and no galling. This was a material limit at 3.2mm, not a mechanism problem.
>
> Which is the good news, because the mechanism is the part we cannot change in six weeks.
