## Why this exists

We switched the outer layer to Meniscus-C polymer on 27 August. Polymer arrives
with a different set of defects than glass did. Glass came in either perfect or
cracked. Polymer arrives with haze, orange peel in the hard coat, and edge
curl from the roll, and none of those are visible in the box.

This runbook covers every incoming lot until we have four clean lots in a row,
then we move to sampling.

## What you need

| Item | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Raking light lamp | Inspection bench |
| Haze meter | Optical bench, calibrate weekly |
| Flat granite plate | Inspection bench |
| Feeler gauge set | Inspection bench |
| Lot traveller form | Print from the quality folder |

## Steps

1. Record the lot number, the vendor date code and the received quantity on the
   traveller. A lot with no date code is quarantined, no exceptions.
2. Acclimatise the carton in the lab for four hours before opening. Polymer
   coming out of a cold truck will read curl that is not really there.
3. Take a sample of ten panels from three different positions in the carton.
4. On each sample panel, measure haze on the haze meter at three points. Record
   all three. Limit is 0.9 percent, and any single reading over that fails the
   panel.
5. Lay each sample panel on the granite plate, coating side up, and measure edge
   curl with the feeler gauges at the four corners. Limit is 0.4 mm.
6. Inspect the hard coat under raking light at a shallow angle. You are looking
   for orange peel, which reads as a texture in the reflection. Grade it
   cosmetic, watch or stop, and put your name on anything graded watch.
7. Do the coin drag on one panel per lot. Two passes at 500 g. A mark you can
   catch with a fingernail is a stop.
8. Sign the traveller and file it. Attach the haze readings as a table of
   numbers so the trend is readable across lots.
9. If any panel fails, quarantine the whole lot and tell Dana the same day.
   Dana handles the vendor conversation from there.

> Four clean lots in a row moves us to sampling at one carton in five. We are at
> two as of 6 September.

**What if the haze meter is out of calibration?**
Stop. Do not inspect against an uncalibrated meter and write down numbers that
look like data. Calibrate first, the standard takes ten minutes.

**Can I use the old glass inspection form?**
No. The glass form has no haze or curl line and it has a crack check that does
not apply. Print the polymer traveller.
