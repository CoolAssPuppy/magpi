SCOPE

The client requested three separate drop programmes on a pre-production folding
device and its accessories. The device is described by the client as a
four-panel, three-hinge handset, mass 289 g, folded envelope 168.0 x 74.4 x
15.8 mm at the thick end and 14.1 mm at the thin end.

  Programme 1   Device in the moulded fibre retail pack, transit and handling
  Programme 2   Device in the folio case, bare drops, three fold states
  Programme 3   Desk stand, bare drops and device-seated topple

The client is advised that no programme in this report constitutes a device
qualification. Devices supplied were pre-production B-series units and results
are indicative of the current build only.

SAMPLES AS RECEIVED

  Devices          B14, B15, B16, B17 (four)
  Folio cases      Six, Chalk finish, vendor sample one
  Desk stands      Four, Chalk, printed prototype, not production tooling
  Retail packs     Twelve, moulded fibre, tray revision 2, sleeve, wallet
  Ballast units    Eight, mass and stiffness matched, no display

  Note. Client supplied only four functional devices. Programmes were sequenced
  so that ballast units absorbed the destructive drops and functional devices
  were used only where a post-drop functional check was required.

PROGRAMME 1  PACKAGED DEVICE

Method       ISTA 3A, small parcel, single parcel
Conditioning 12 hours at 23 C, 50 percent RH
Drop height  760 mm, packaged mass under 9.5 kg
Sequence     One corner, three edges, six faces
Packs        Six packs, three with ballast, three with functional devices

Table 1. Packaged drop results

  Pack   Content     Orientation of worst damage   Device outcome
  P-01   Ballast     Hinge-1 end face              tray cracked, unit retained
  P-02   Ballast     Corner, hinge-1 end           tray cracked, unit retained
  P-03   Ballast     Flat face, sleeve down        no damage
  P-04   B14         Hinge-1 end face              pass, no functional change
  P-05   B15         Corner, hinge-1 end           pass, cosmetic frame mark
  P-06   B16         Edge, long, tray floor down   pass, no functional change

Table 2. Tray condition after the full sequence

  Feature                      P-01    P-02    P-03    P-04    P-05    P-06
  Hinge-1 end wall             crack   crack   intact  crack   crack   intact
  Compliant rib, panel 4 edge  intact  intact  intact  intact  intact  intact
  Cavity floor, wedge section  intact  intact  intact  intact  intact  intact
  Device retained in cavity    yes     yes     yes     yes     yes     yes

  Observation. The hinge-1 end wall cracked in four of six packs and in every
  pack subjected to a drop on that end. The wall is 1.4 mm and is the tallest
  section of the tray, because it is the end that carries the thick side of the
  wedge. The crack propagates from the cavity floor radius upward. In no case
  did the device leave the cavity and in no case did the crack reach the outer
  sleeve.

  The client's stated clearance at this end is 1.2 mm. The tray therefore
  arrests the device over a shorter distance than at the thin end, which is
  consistent with the damage being concentrated there.

  Recommendation. Increase the end wall radius at the cavity floor and add a
  crush rib normal to the end wall. A crack that retains the device is not a
  transit failure, but a cracked tray visible on unboxing is a cosmetic finding
  and the client indicated this matters.

  Note on D-2. Ballast unit only. The unit folded about hinge 2 on impact and
  the two halves closed on each other. The case spine did not resist the
  motion. The client elected not to repeat this drop with a functional device
  and the outcome is recorded as a fail against the ballast criterion of no
  uncommanded state change. The laboratory observes that a 656 mm object
  dropped flat has a high moment about its centre hinge and that no case
  material at the thickness supplied would resist it.

  Note on D-1. Hinge 2 measured stiff after the drop and recovered to within
  the client's stated range after approximately fifty manual cycles. The client
  attributes this to lubricant displacement and has an open internal item.

  Case fit observation, recorded at client request

  All six cases supplied were checked for free travel through the three fold
  states before testing. Six of six bound before flat when moving from tablet
  to desk, at 38 to 44 degrees short of flat, measured at hinge 3. This is a
  fit finding and not a drop finding. It is recorded here because it affected
  the test method: desk state drops required the case to be held flat by an
  external strap during setup, and the strap was removed before release.

  Cases were also inspected for contact with the outer display layer. All six
  showed a contact witness along the panel 1 outer edge after fifty open and
  close cycles, before any drop. Under a point source the witness is visible on
  the display layer of B17 as a faint line approximately 60 mm long. It did not
  grow during the drop programme.

SUMMARY OF FINDINGS

  1. Packaged transit passes ISTA 3A with the device retained in all cases. The
     tray hinge-1 end wall cracks on end drops and is a cosmetic finding.

  2. The folio case provides no measurable protection benefit in the folded and
     tablet states at 1000 mm, because the device passed those orientations
     without it in the client's own prior programme. The case does not make
     anything worse.

  3. The desk state with the case fitted is the weakest configuration tested.
     The device is long, the mass is distributed to the ends, and the moment
     about hinge 2 exceeds anything the case spine can carry.

  4. Case contact with the outer display layer was observed on all six samples
     before any drop and is the finding the laboratory would rank first.

  5. Desk stand results are indicative only. Retest on production tooling.

LIMITATIONS

Pre-production samples. Four functional devices. Single-sample orientations in
programmes 2 and 3. No temperature or humidity extremes. No repeated-drop
sequences on a single unit except where stated. This report describes the
behaviour of the samples supplied and does not predict field performance.
