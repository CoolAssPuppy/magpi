## How to use this

Every answer here is the answer. Say it the way it is written. If you think one
is wrong, change it here rather than saying a better version in a room and
leaving the page alone.

## Product

**What is the Fold S1?**
A phone that opens into a work surface. Four panels, three hinges, pocket sized
when it is shut.

**Why four panels?**
Two panels give you a slightly bigger phone. Four give you a surface the size of
a desk mat. The surface is the point.

**Does the software do anything special?**
It knows what shape the device is in and lays your work out for that shape.
Shut, it is a phone. Fully open, it is three apps side by side. Propped at 90
degrees, the lower half becomes a keyboard.

**Is this a tablet or a phone?**
It is a phone. It replaces the tablet you were also carrying.

## Durability

**Will it crease?**
The outer layer is a polymer selected for how it behaves at the bend radius the
device actually uses, and we test against a 200,000 cycle spec, which is a two
year life at normal use. Any specific test result goes through Sam before it is
said in public.

**Is the outer screen glass?**
It is a polymer. Say it plainly.

**Is a polymer weaker than glass?**
It is softer, which means it is more forgiving of being folded and less
resistant to being scratched. We made that trade on purpose. Do not volunteer
a hardness number.

**What about dust in the hinges?**
Covered by the sealing work. Do not make a rating claim until certification is
finished.

## Availability and price

**When can I buy it?**
Availability comes at launch. The date is under embargo and it is not said in
any room that has not signed one.

**What does it cost?**
Pricing comes at launch. The number is not in marketing documents and John owns
it until it goes into the press materials.

**Where can I buy it?**
Through carriers and directly. Two carrier partners are signed and each will
announce on its own schedule.
