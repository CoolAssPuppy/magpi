## Description

The sleeve carries the colourway name in type on the front and again on the end flap. Four variants, four files, one printer, one slot. Once the file is released we are committed to those four words.

Ink, Chalk, Moss and Ember came out of the 24 August review. Nobody has signed them. Everyone behaves as though they are signed, which is the state right before somebody changes one.

What this issue needs: a written sign off from Maya and Jane on the four names, dated, in the Notion page, before the art goes out. Not a thread, not a nod in a sync.
