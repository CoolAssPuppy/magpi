Eleven markets, three tiers. A tier is a date, not a ranking. Tier 2 markets are
not worse markets, they are markets where something we do not control has to
finish first.

## The tiers

| Tier | Markets | When | Committed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Korea, Japan, United States, Germany, United Kingdom | Launch day, 4 November 2026 | Yes |
| 2 | France, Netherlands, Canada, Australia | Six weeks after, 16 December 2026 | Yes |
| 3 | Singapore, United Arab Emirates | 2027 | No date |

## Why these five are tier 1

| Market | The reason | The gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Korea | Highest folding intent in the panel, by a distance. Also where our second display vendor lives. | Carrier A certification |
| Japan | Second highest intent, and the market with the strongest response to the desk state. | Carrier B certification |
| United States | Largest single volume, and the market the press writes from. | FCC Part 15B |
| Germany | Largest European volume and the strictest retail returns culture we will meet. | CE, distributor onboarding |
| United Kingdom | Small volume, high review density. We launch here for the coverage as much as the units. | Carrier B, UKCA |

## Why tier 2 is six weeks and not twelve

Six weeks is the shortest gap that lets a local language build ship without a
second firmware branch. Ben's number, not a marketing number. French and Dutch
localisation, Canadian bilingual packaging and the Australian power adapter all
land in the same window, and none of them is on the critical path for tier 1.

The six week gap also buys us one full replenishment cycle of tier 1 data before
we commit the tier 2 channel fill. If the mix is wrong in Germany we find out in
time to change what we send to France.

## Tier 3 and why there is no date on it

Singapore and the United Arab Emirates are both small, both willing, and both
require regulatory work we have not started. IMDA and TDRA each want their own
type approval, and the UAE wants an Arabic first-run experience that does not
exist in the Ori shell yet.

> There is no committed tier 3 date and nobody should write one down. If a
> partner in either market asks, the answer is 2027 and we will come back to
> them with a quarter when we have one.

## The forecast this feeds

First quarter, tiers 1 and 2 together, 180,000 units. Korea and Japan are about
40 percent of that between them, which is why the panel work weights those two
and why the colourway mix is not the same in every market.

The full build is in the regional demand model. This page is the tier list and
the reasons. When a number changes, it changes there first.
