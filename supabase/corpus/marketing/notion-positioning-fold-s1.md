## The one sentence

The Fold S1 is a phone that opens into a work surface, for people whose work has
never fitted on a phone.

## Who it is for

| Segment | What they do today | What changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Field professionals | Carry a phone and a tablet, use the phone, resent the tablet | One device, four panels, the tablet stops being a second thing to carry |
| People who read for a living | Read on a phone badly or a laptop reluctantly | Three panels open is a book, and it fits in a pocket shut |
| Anyone in back to back meetings | Notes on a laptop, calls on a phone, neither at the same time | LAPTOP posture, notes and the call on one device |
| Creators on location | Phone plus a laptop for review | DESK posture is a review surface at the shoot |

We are not chasing the person who wants a novelty. Folding phones have been sold
as a marvel for years and the category has stayed small because a marvel is a
thing you look at once.

## The argument

> The four panel surface is the product. The folding is how it gets into your
> pocket.

Everything in the messaging pushes on what the open device lets you do. A
two panel folding phone gives you a slightly bigger phone. Four panels give you
a surface the size of a desk mat, and that is a different category of thing.

The order of the argument, and we hold this order everywhere:

1. It opens into a work surface.
2. It fits in your pocket when it is shut.
3. The software knows which shape it is in and lays the work out for you.
4. It survives being opened and closed for years.

Point four sits fourth on purpose. Durability is the question every reviewer
asks and the answer is defensive. Leading with it makes the whole pitch sound
like an apology.

## Proof points we can use

| Claim | Proof | Cleared |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Four panels, three hinges | The device | Yes |
| Fits in a pocket | Folded thickness | Yes |
| Software adapts to the shape | Six panel states in the shell | Yes |
| Built for years of folding | Cycle testing to a 200,000 cycle spec | Only once the current test run finishes |

**Can we say the outer layer is glass?**
No. It is a polymer as of the 27 August decision and any material claim goes
through Sam before it goes in a deck.

**Do we talk about the hinge count?**
Yes, three hinges is concrete and nobody else has it. Do not name the hinge
assembly in public. Bellows is an internal word.

## What we do not say

- No comparison to a named competitor device in owned channels.
- No claim about the display material's hardness. We changed material and the
  hardness number moved with it.
- No unit numbers, no production volumes, no line locations.
