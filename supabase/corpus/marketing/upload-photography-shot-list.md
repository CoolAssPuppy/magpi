ON THE UNITS

Two units, cosmetically perfect, that hold a half fold position for five days
without drifting. Half the shot list is the device standing at an angle on a
desk and if it sags over ten minutes we lose the shot. The stiffer detent
spring went into the hardware build in early September, so a September unit
holds its angle far better than the one the photographer tried in August.

Anybody who touches a unit wears gloves. The outer layer is a polymer and it
marks. A scratch that appears on day two is on every frame from day two.

THE FOLD LINE RULE

The fold line appears once, deliberately, in the print work, at a size where
nobody can accuse us of hiding it. Everywhere else we light to the product and
whatever the light does is what the light does.

No lighting setup is to be chosen for the purpose of making the fold line
disappear. If a setup does that by accident, note it in the log and keep the
frame, and we will decide later.

A NOTE FROM ENGINEERING THAT AFFECTS SHOT 12

Panels with no content change for forty-five seconds dim slightly and ramp back
up when something moves. On a long exposure of four static panels this will
show as uneven brightness across the device. Ben can supply a build with the
behaviour turned off for the shoot. Ask him at least three days before the
shoot day and do not ask him on the morning.

WHAT WE ARE NOT SHOOTING

No mountain. No coffee shop. No slow motion. No hands from below. No blue
gradient. No stock model looking delighted at a screen.
