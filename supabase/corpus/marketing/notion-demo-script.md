## What the demo has to prove

One thing. The device knows what shape it is in and puts your work where it
belongs. Everything else in the launch materials is a claim about hardware, and
this is the one moment where software has to carry the room.

> Do not narrate the panel states. Nobody outside this company knows what DESK
> means and telling them makes the demo about vocabulary.

## The run

| Time | Action | What is said |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 | Device shut in hand, a message arrives | "Shut, it is a phone. This is the whole outer screen." |
| 0:30 | Open one panel, reply to the message | "One fold, and it is the phone you already know." |
| 1:15 | Open the second panel, message and map side by side | "Two folds. Two things at once, no juggling." |
| 2:15 | Open the third panel, three apps | "Fully open, it is a desk." |
| 3:15 | Close back to two panels mid task | "It follows you back down. Nothing is lost." |
| 4:00 | Prop at 90 degrees, keyboard appears on the lower half | "Set it down and it is a laptop." |
| 5:00 | Shut it, put it in a pocket | "And then it goes in a pocket." |
| 5:30 | Hold, silence | Nothing. Let the room look at it shut. |

## Rehearsal notes

- The settle timer means every transition takes a beat to commit. Move
  deliberately. Fast hands make the shell look slow.
- Entering the 90 degree posture takes 400 milliseconds before the keyboard
  appears. That pause is intentional and rushing past it on stage looks like a
  bug.
- Do not demonstrate the propped video posture. Detection is inferred from the
  accelerometer and it is unreliable when anything is moving.
- Have a second unit within reach, charged, on the same firmware build.

**What if the shell crashes on stage?**
Shut the device, count to five, open it. That is the whole recovery and it
looks like nothing happened. Do not plug anything in and do not explain.

**Which build do we demo on?**
Whatever is the last tagged build at rehearsal, frozen from that point. Not the
newest one. A build nobody has rehearsed on is a risk with no upside.

**Can we show the compatibility letterbox?**
No. An app that does not use the full surface is the least interesting thing the
device does.
