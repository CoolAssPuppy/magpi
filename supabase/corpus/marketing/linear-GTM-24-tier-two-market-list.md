## Description

Tier 1 is locked at five. Tier 2 has been "four or five European and
Commonwealth markets" in every document since July and that is not a list.

Candidates on the table: France, Netherlands, Spain, Italy, Sweden, Canada,
Australia.

Pick four. The constraints are that each one needs a localisation build inside
the six week window, a distributor or carrier who will take a first generation
device, and a returns process we can staff with seven people.

Deliverable is a list of four with a one line reason each, in the market tiers
page, by 4 September.
