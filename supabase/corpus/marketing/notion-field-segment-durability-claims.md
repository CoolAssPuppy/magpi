Field is thirteen percent of the panel and the lowest intent of the six
segments at 38.9 percent. It is also the only segment that told us exactly what
would change its mind. This page is the list of what we are allowed to say back.

Nothing on this page goes into a deck, a page or a script until Sam has signed
the specific wording. Not the claim, the wording. A claim that is true and a
sentence that is true are two different objects and we have already learned
that once this quarter.

## What they actually asked

Straight from the open ends in the table volume, because paraphrasing these
loses the thing that makes them useful.

> "I would want to see it after a year in a work bag. Not a new one. A used
> one." DE-0233, 46

> "Every folding phone I have held has a bump in the middle you can feel with
> your thumb. Has this one got a bump. Yes or no." UK-0301, 38

> "Three hinges is three things that can break. Show me one that has been open
> and shut for two years and I will look again." DE-0188, 41

> "The line down the middle. I have seen it on other folding phones and it never
> goes away and it always gets worse." US-0092, 44

None of those four is a question about toughness. They are questions about time,
about a specific physical sensation, and about what happens to a used device.
Every durability line we have written so far answers a question nobody asked.

### The cycle number

The specification is 200,000 folds, which is about 270 a day for two years. That
assumption has to travel with the number every single time. A number without its
assumption is the kind of claim a reviewer takes apart in one paragraph.

The polymer run restarted from zero when the material changed on 27 August. Until
that run finishes we describe the specification and not a result. The difference
between "built to a 200,000 cycle specification" and "tested to 200,000 cycles"
is the whole page and both Sam and Jane have said so.

### The crease

The outer layer is a polymer as of the 27 August decision and it creases less
than the glass did. We measure it on a gloss meter at sixty degrees against a
2.5 GU gate, with three people looking at the panel behind that as a veto.

We can say it does not crease. We do not publish the gate number, because 2.5 GU
means nothing to a buyer and everything to a competitor.

**Can we answer the bump question yes or no?**
Not yet. The gloss gate measures what a fold line looks like. It does not measure
what a thumb feels, and UK-0301 asked about a thumb. Priya to ask Sam whether
there is a surface measurement that answers it, and if there is not, whether
there could be by the time we are writing reviewer material.

### The scratch trade

The polymer marks more easily than glass would have. This is true, it is known
inside the company, and it is going to be in every review.

The instruction from the 27 August call, in Sam's words, was to say whatever is
true. It does not crease. It scratches more than glass. Both are true and one of
them is easier to live with. For Field specifically, we say both, in that order,
without a softening clause between them. This segment can tell when it is being
managed.

### The used unit

The single strongest request in the segment and the one we are least ready for.

Field wants to see a device that has been used, not a device that is new. We do
not have one, because every unit older than two weeks is a glass unit and glass
units are props now. B11 and B12 are the first polymer builds and their counters
started at zero.

Proposal: ask for one polymer unit to be run to a published cycle count and then
photographed, unretouched, next to a new one. That is a hardware request with a
schedule cost and it is not mine to make. Written here so it is on a page rather
than in my head.

## What we do not do

- No adjective where a number will fit.
- No durability claim in a headline. Field enters the argument at point four,
  which means the durability answer is the door, not the poster.
- No warranty language until somebody who is not in marketing has read it.
- No photograph of a device that has been polished for the shot and described as
  used.
