## Embargo rules

- Every briefed party signs before they see anything.
- Review units are numbered and tracked to a person.
- No asset with the launch date on it leaves this group without Maya sending it.
- If the date leaks, we do not confirm and we do not deny. Jane speaks.

**What happens if a carrier moves its date?**
Our date does not move. The carrier announcements hang off ours and the plan
above is written so either carrier can slip a day without touching launch day.

**Can engineering see this page?**
The people who need the date have it. Anybody asking for a date because they are
planning a build should be talking to Dana about the pilot line schedule, which
has its own dates and does not need this one.
