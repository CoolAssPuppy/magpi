## Description

The desk state is 21.9 percent of expected daily use across the whole panel and it is 45.9 percent of the Creator segment's answer. Creator is 18 percent of the base and 46 percent of everyone who named the desk state. No other segment is close.

The film currently in the Fieldhouse treatment is one continuous take, one pair of hands, a desk, no music for the first eleven seconds. That treatment is good and it is not cast. It is written for a general audience and the general audience does not expect to use the shape the film is about.

Ask: rewrite the brief so the film is explicitly a Creator film. Twenty two to twenty nine, the desk state, a real editing task on the panels, shot in a place a person in that segment recognises. The panel gave us the shot list in the open ends without meaning to.

> KR-0355, 22
> "Open all the way, timeline on the bottom, preview on top. If it does that on a train I never open my laptop again."

Two dependencies flagged up front:

- Creator indexes 125 in Korea and 114 in Japan and 74 in both Germany and the United Kingdom. If the film is a Creator film then the film is a Korea and Japan asset first and the European cut is a different edit.
- Moss is the Creator finish. It is the only segment where a finish beats Ink, and it beats Ink in the two markets Creator is concentrated in. The hero unit in this film should be Moss, which is not what GTM-18 is currently sourcing.

Not started. Blocked on whether the shell can hold the layout in the shot for the length of a continuous take.
