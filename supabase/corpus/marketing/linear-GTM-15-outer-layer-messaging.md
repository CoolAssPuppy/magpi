## Description

Engineering changed the outer layer material at the end of August. Two of our decks, the website draft and the sizzle script all say glass. Every one of those has to change before anything goes to a carrier or a reviewer.

Also: we need an answer for the question a reviewer will ask, which is why a phone at this price does not use glass on the outside.
