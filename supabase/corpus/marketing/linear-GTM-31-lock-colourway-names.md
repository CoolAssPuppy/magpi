## Description

Four finishes ship: Ink, Chalk, Moss, Ember. Those are the words industrial design has used since the review on 24 August and nobody has ever decided whether they are also the public names.

The name goes printed on the sleeve, not on a sticker, so it is locked when the packaging art goes to print. After that a name change is a reprint of every sleeve in every finish in eleven markets.

Deliverable: four public names, one line of copy under each, and a written answer to why there are four and not six.
