## Description

Both carriers have given verbal sell-in numbers for the first quarter and
neither has signed a schedule with finishes broken out. Dana cannot order
coating capacity against a verbal number.

What we need in writing from each of them:

1. Total first quarter commitment, in units, by market.
2. The split between the launch tranche and replenishment.
3. Units by finish, because Ember and Moss have different lead times.
4. The date after which the numbers stop moving.

Target is both schedules signed by 30 September, which is ten working days
before the asset deadline and four weeks before Dana's coating commit.
