## The material reversal, how I plan to tell it

The honest version, because the board will hear it better than a tidy one. On 12
August we chose ultra thin glass for the outer layer on the strength of a vendor
cycle claim and a clean sample at 120,000 cycles. On 24 August a test unit showed
a line at 150,000 cycles. On 27 August that unit fractured across the fold at
180,000 against a 200,000 cycle spec, and we reversed to a polymer the same day.

The reason it was the same day and not the following week is that John flagged
the tooling commit deadline on the Wednesday. A week later, the same decision
costs a mask set.

> The lesson I want on the slide is about the calendar. A
> small company's real advantage is that a decision can move in a day. That only
> works when the person holding the deadline speaks before the deadline.
