Pigment is 0.48 a unit on both plans, to the nearest cent, because pigment
follows units and the units did not change. All of the delta is setup. A colour
costs money to start and almost nothing to continue, which is the whole
economics of this in one line.

The coating line was never the problem. The anodising line has nine passes in
the window and a second pass costs a slot the same as a first one, which is why
one oxide red finish at 6,800 units was competing with Chalk for a slot. Four
finishes uses eight of the nine and leaves one spare.
