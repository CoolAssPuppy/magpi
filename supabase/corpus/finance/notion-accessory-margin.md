## Two products

A folio case in each of the four shipping finishes, and a desk stand in Chalk
only. That is the whole accessory line and I would like to keep it that way.

| | Price | Unit cost | Margin | Margin percent |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Folio case | $79 | $27.40 | $51.60 | 65.3 percent |
| Desk stand | $129 | $58.20 | $70.80 | 54.9 percent |

Both are well above the device at 40.0 percent, which is normal and which is why
every phone company sells cases.

## What it is worth

Attach rates are the soft number here. I have used 34 percent on the folio and 7
percent on the stand, taken from two published foldable launches and discounted
for the fact that we have no installed base and no retail floor of our own.

| | Attach | Units | Revenue | Gross margin |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Folio case | 34 percent | 61,200 | $4,834,800 | $3,157,920 |
| Desk stand | 7 percent | 12,600 | $1,625,400 | $892,080 |
| **Total** | | **73,800** | **$6,460,200** | **$4,050,000** |

$6,460,200 is 1.9 percent of the $341,820,000 of device revenue and $4,050,000
is 2.9 percent of the gross margin. Blended with the device, the first quarter
comes out at $348,280,200 of revenue and $140,670,000 of margin, which is 40.4
percent instead of 40.0.

Four tenths of a point. Worth having and not worth building a business unit
around.

## Attach rate sensitivity

| Folio attach | Units | Revenue | Gross margin |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 25 percent | 45,000 | $3,555,000 | $2,322,000 |
| 34 percent | 61,200 | $4,834,800 | $3,157,920 |
| 45 percent | 81,000 | $6,399,000 | $4,179,600 |

The spread between the low and high case is $1,857,600 of margin, which is more
than the whole desk stand line. If anyone wants to spend effort on accessories,
it goes into folio attach and not into a third product.

## The colour problem, again

Folio tooling is $19,500 per finish and it does not care how many cases come off
it. Amortised over the folio volume in each finish, it is a different number in
each finish.

| Finish | Folios | Tooling per folio | Margin after tooling |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ink | 23,203 | $0.84 | $50.76 |
| Chalk | 16,363 | $1.19 | $50.41 |
| Moss | 13,666 | $1.43 | $50.17 |
| Ember | 7,968 | $2.45 | $49.15 |
| **Blended** | **61,200** | **$1.27** | **$50.33** |

Ember absorbs nearly three times the tooling per folio that Ink does and still
returns $49.15 a case. Colour count does not threaten this line, it just makes
the smallest finish slightly less good than the largest one.

The vendor minimum is the constraint that does bite. 5,000 pieces per finish,
and Ember at 7,968 clears it with 2,968 to spare. A sixth finish at 6,800
devices would have been about 2,300 folios, so we would have bought 5,000 and
scrapped more than half of them. Dana found that in OPS-71 and it is the
cleanest argument against a sixth colour in the whole file.

## Why the desk stand is Chalk only

Because four stands need four tools.

| | Tooling | Units | Tooling per unit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Chalk only | $34,000 | 12,600 | $2.70 |
| All four finishes | $136,000 | 12,600 | $10.79 |

Same 12,600 units either way, split four ways instead of one. Tooling per unit
goes from $2.70 to $10.79 and margin per stand drops from $68.10 to $60.01, and
the smallest run would be about 3,150 pieces against a vendor minimum I have not
even asked about yet.

A desk stand sits on a desk under a phone. Nobody is looking at the stand.
