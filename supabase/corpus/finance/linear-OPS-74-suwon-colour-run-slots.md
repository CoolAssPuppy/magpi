## Description

Eight coating runs to cover 180,000 units across four finishes. Suwon holds 14 slots across October, November, December and January and other customers are asking for them. Book the eight, get them in writing, and work out what a slot costs us if we book it and do not use it.

Runs are sized on pieces, which is devices plus the 4 percent lamination scrap allowance. A run tops out at 26,000 pieces.
