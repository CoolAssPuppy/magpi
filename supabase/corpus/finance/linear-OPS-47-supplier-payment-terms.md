## Description

We are a seven person company buying from a vendor whose other customers order in millions. Their standard terms are 30 days net and a 12 week firm order horizon, which for us means committing cash to material three months before we sell a phone.

Two things to negotiate:

1. Terms out to 60 days, or a deposit structure that does not require the full order value up front.
2. Buffer stock held at the vendor as a contractual obligation rather than as a favour, given that they are currently the only source for the outer layer.
