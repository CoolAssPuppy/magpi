## Shape of the year

Seven people, one product, one launch. Roughly two thirds of the spend in this
plan is either payroll or things that have to happen before a device can be
sold. The discretionary part of the budget is small enough that arguing about it

## What is not in here

- Returns and warranty reserve. It belongs in the model and it is not there yet.
- Any second source qualification for the hinge assembly, because there is no
  quote to put in a line.
- A second lamination fixture. One fixture over 100,000 units adds $2.14 to unit
  cost and two adds $4.28, and two shifts in January instead of March. That
  decision waits for a real yield number from the pilot build.
- Volume production. This plan covers the pilot line only.

**Why is marketing only 10 percent?**
Because the launch plan is a small number of briefings and a set of carrier
assets rather than a media spend. If that changes, it changes by a factor and
not by a percent, and it would need a conversation with Jane rather than a line
edit here.

**What happens if certification slips?**
Certification and test is the smallest line that can hurt us. A failed pass at
the test house means a rebook, and rebooking is about $40,000 and four weeks.
The four weeks is the expensive part.

**Is payroll going up in this plan?**
No new headcount is planned in FY27. Two contractor engagements end in the first
half and are not renewed in this draft.
