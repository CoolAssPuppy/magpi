# Hiring plan, second half of 2026

Owner: Diane Ockley
Date: 5 June 2026
Status: agreed with Marcus. Not yet shared outside this space
Circulation: leadership. Ade has the cost lines

Five roles between July and December. That takes us from 31 people to 36, which
is the largest single move we have made in three years and I want the reasoning
written down before anybody sees a job advert.

Nothing on this page is announced. Two of these roles are visible to the person
whose team they sit in and three are not, and I would rather tell people in the
right order than have them read a careers page.

## The five

| 1 | Engineer, billing and platform | Priya | Engineer 3 | £68,000 to £82,000 | 1 September |
| 2 | Account executive | Elena | AE 2 | £44,000 to £52,000 base, £74,000 to £88,000 on target | 1 September |

candidate needs an exception, that is a conversation with me and not a decision
the hiring manager makes on a Friday afternoon.

## Why each one

**1. Engineer, billing and platform.** Jonah moves to the search team on 1 July.
That is the right move and I signed it off, and it leaves Priya as the only
person who has read the billing service end to end, on the quarter when the
billing service is the largest piece of work we have.

Priya inherited that code and did not ask for it. She has said so in every
conversation I have had with her since January and picked it up anyway. Hiring
one person into it is the difference between a lead who owns a service and a
lead who is a single point of failure with a title.

Marcus and Priya are reviewing the March billing decision this month. The
outcome of that review changes what this person works on. It does not change
whether we need them.

**2. Account executive.** Elena has four account managers and is carrying
renewals herself on top of running the team. The October renewal window and the
pricing work land in the same eight weeks, and the current plan has Elena
personally handling the accounts where the timing is awkward. That is fine for a
handful and it is not a structure.

This is the role with the clearest payback and it is also the one I am least
worried about filling.

**3. Infrastructure engineer.** Kenji is one person. He is the escalation for
everything that is the cloud rather than the product, he owns the audit log
rewrite, he owns the region work after it, and he cannot go on the primary on
call rota because he is the person you escalate to.

If Kenji is ill for two weeks in October we find out how much of our
infrastructure is in one person's head. I do not want to find out.

**4. Support engineer.** Hal and Rosa are two people answering everything.
Support headcount has not moved since 2024 while accounts have. Hal has never
once asked me for this, which is part of why I am putting it on the list.

**5. Product designer, fixed term.** Ruth's parental leave starts in November.
Ruth is our only designer. A twelve month fixed term contract with a four week
overlap before she goes is the whole plan and the overlap is the part that
matters, so the start date is not flexible even if the others are.

## The order we fill them in

1. Billing and platform engineer. Open now, first.
2. Designer cover, in parallel, because the date is fixed by Ruth's leave and
   not by us.
3. Account executive.
4. Infrastructure engineer.
5. Support engineer.

Three and four are close together and I expect them to overlap in practice. Five
is last on purpose: it is the one that can start in January without anything
breaking, and I would rather hold it and be honest about holding it than open it
and quietly not fill it.

No recruiters except the infrastructure role, where we have tried twice on our
own and got nowhere. Every other role is our own network and the careers page,
which is how we hired everybody currently here.

## Cost

Ade has the detail in the H2 budget. The summary is that the five roles cost
roughly £96,000 in the second half of the year because of the start dates, and
roughly £330,000 in a full year once they are all in place, including employer
costs and the agency fee on role three.

The full year number is the one that matters and it is the one that is easy to
skip past when you are looking at a half year budget.

## If revenue is under plan

I would rather write the rule now than argue about it in September with a
candidate in a process.

The check is at the end of August, against the revenue plan Ade and I agreed in
January.

- **Under plan by up to four percent.** Everything proceeds. That is inside the
  range we planned for.
- **Under by four to eight percent.** Roles 3 and 4 hold until January. The
  infrastructure role hurts to hold and it is the one that can be held, because
  the risk it removes is a risk we have already been carrying for two years.
  Roles 1, 2 and 5 proceed regardless.
- **Under by more than eight percent.** Roles 1 and 5 only. Role 1 because the
  billing work cannot be done by one person and role 5 because it is cover for a
  leave, not growth.

Roles 1 and 5 do not get held under any circumstance on this page. If we get to
a point where they need holding, that is a different conversation and it is not
a hiring conversation.

Anyone already in a process when a hold takes effect gets told immediately and
told the truth. We do not slow walk people.

## What is not on this list

A second product manager. Sofia has asked twice, and it is the strongest case I
have said no to this year. The reason is that the four things on the Q3 plan
have owners and a second product manager arriving in Q3 makes five things in
flight, which is the opposite of what Marcus has been asking for since last
autumn.

Revisit in January.
