Notes. Elena, 14 Aug.

Diane asked me on Tuesday which renewals worry me. This is the answer and it is
longer than she wanted.

Rules I used, so you can argue with them rather than with my feelings. High
means I think there is a real chance we lose it or have to discount it. Medium
means it will take work and I expect to win. Low means I would be surprised.
Annual value is what they billed us over the last twelve months including
overage, not the plan fee, because the plan fee is not what these accounts cost
them and it is not what they will be looking at.

The four things I would like decided before September, in the order they will
bite.

**One. The sixty day problem.** If we announce a price change in September, then
every account renewing before roughly the start of December cannot be given
sixty days notice before their renewal date. That is Rutherford, Calderwood,
Ostler, Marchetti, Halvorsen, Hartwell and Penrhyn. Seven accounts, $114,020.

I do not think we can move those accounts to a new price at those renewals. I
think they renew at today's price and move at the renewal after. If somebody
tells me otherwise I would like it in writing, because I am the one making the
phone calls and I am not going to improvise a rule that I then have to defend in
March.

Rutherford is the easy version of this. They renew before anything is announced.
Twelve months at today's price, they move in August next year, and nobody has to
have a difficult conversation. I would like all seven to look like Rutherford.

**Two. Three accounts I want held at today's price for a year, regardless of
when they renew.**

Bergstrom. They committed to us on the EU region during their March renewal
conversation and we slipped it. Their compliance person told me in June that the
thing they cannot live with is a date that moves twice. It has not moved twice
yet. If the first thing they hear after a slip is a price rise, I lose the
credit I got for telling them early, and that credit is the only reason that
conversation went well.

Marchetti. Two billing errors on their invoices in May, both ours, both fixed,
both apologised for. Also an EU account. Also October.

Halvorsen. Biggest account we have, renewing inside the window, and on the old
master services agreement, which is a document I would rather not have anybody
reading closely in the same month we change a price.

I am not asking for a discount. I am asking to leave three accounts alone for
twelve months.

**Three. Ostler.** This one is not about pricing and it is the one I would
actually lose sleep over. Their champion left in June. The new operations
director came from a company that ran a full TMS and she has said the word
"lightweight" to me twice, and she did not mean it kindly. They renew on 30
September, which is before any announcement, so at least the two problems do not
arrive together.

I have a session booked with her on the 3rd. If that goes badly I will say so
rather than manage it quietly until October.

**Four. Port Ellery has no owner.** It went to Marek and Marek left in April and
nobody picked it up. A fifteen thousand dollar account has had no account
manager for four months and I found that out building this sheet. That is mine
and I am fixing it this week, and it is also the argument for the account
executive role, so I am leaving it in the file rather than quietly correcting
it.

Two things not in the sheet.

The six accounts on an annual commitment paid monthly hold their current rate
until the commitment ends whatever we decide. Two of them are in this list,
Penrhyn and Tarrant. That is contractual and it is not a negotiation.

And I still do not have the numbers. I know why. I am not going to be able to
answer Hartwell in September without them, and she has told me she is doing her
budget in September, so at some point the confidentiality and the renewal
calendar are going to collide and somebody other than me has to decide which one
wins.
