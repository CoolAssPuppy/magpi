# Interview notes, backend candidate, 16 July

Tomas. 45 min, product and collaboration slot. Priya had him before me, Nadia
after.

## Good

Explained a queue backlog he had chased for a week and did not once make himself
the hero of it. Said the fix was boring and said what the boring fix was. I like
people who can describe a week of being wrong.

Asked me what happens when support disagrees with product about priority. Not as
a test question. He actually wanted to know, because he has clearly worked
somewhere it went badly.

Wrote almost nothing on the whiteboard and talked through the shape first. Priya
will love that or hate it, no middle.

Reads a spec properly. Caught that my example had two definitions of "open" in
it. Nobody catches that.

## Worried about

Every example he gave was from a team of three or four where he was the person
who decided. Asked him twice about working inside a decision he disagreed with
and both times the story bent back to how he eventually got his way. Second time
he noticed himself doing it, which is something.

We have exactly one open argument like that running right now and I do not want
to hand someone the search disagreement as their first week.

Not a no. A thing to ask his references about.

## My fault

I asked the disagreement question as "tell me about a time you disagreed with a
product manager." Terrible. It tells them who is in the room, it tells them the
shape of the answer I want, and I got the rehearsed one both times.

Stop asking it that way. Ask what the last thing was that they changed their mind
about at work, and then ask who changed it. If nobody changed it, that is the
answer.

Also I talked too much in the first ten minutes. Filled the silence. He was fine
with the silence.

## Lean before debrief

Yes, weak yes, and I want to hear Nadia before I firm it up. If she says he
listened, it is a clean yes. If she says he was explaining search to her, I want
another conversation.

Do not lead the debrief with the worry. Priya will over index on it and Marcus
will read it as me being lukewarm when I am not.
