## Vendor portals

These are per person accounts and they are not in the shared password manager
vault. Ask Dana.

| Portal | What for | Who has an account |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Kyoto vendor portal | UTG-3 glass orders, sample lot requests, datasheets | Sam, Dana |
| Bellows hinge supplier portal | Hinge parts, cam profiles, drawings | Sam, Dana |
| Shenzhen pilot line portal | Line scheduling, build travellers | Dana |
| Test house portal | Certification bookings and reports | Ben, Dana |
| Component library | Datasheets for everything on the bill of materials | Everyone |

The Kyoto vendor portal is the one people ask about most, because the UTG-3
datasheets live there and the outer layer material is the live question this
month. Sam and Dana have accounts. If you need a datasheet, ask rather than
requesting a new account, because the vendor charges per seat.
