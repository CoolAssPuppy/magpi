Everybody keeps asking a version of the same question in a different channel,
so here is one page for the whole company. Eleven markets, three tiers, and a
first quarter forecast of 180,000 units.

This page has no dates on it beyond the month. If you need the day, the people
who plan against the day have it. If you are planning a build, the schedule you
want is Dana's pilot line schedule and not this page.

| Tier | Markets | When |

Tier 1 is 148,500 of it. Tier 2 is 31,500 and gets about seven selling weeks
rather than thirteen, which is why four markets add to less than the United
Kingdom and Germany together.

Korea and Japan are 72,000, or 40 percent, between them. That is not an
accident of the arithmetic. It is where the research says the demand is, and it
is why the colourway mix in those two markets is different from everywhere
else.

Tier 3 is not in the 180,000 and has no approved number.

## Colourways

Four finishes ship. Ink, Chalk, Moss, Ember. Tide and Rust were cut after the
outer layer reversal in August, one for a coating problem and one for a tooling
slot, and neither was a taste decision.

| Finish | Units | Share |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ink | 68,245 | 37.9% |
| Chalk | 48,125 | 26.7% |
| Moss | 40,195 | 22.3% |
| Ember | 23,435 | 13.0% |

Two of those numbers are worth knowing outside marketing.

Chalk is the second largest finish because it shows the crease least of the
four. People chose the finish where they could not find the fold line, which is
the most direct piece of evidence we have that the crease work matters
commercially and not just to Sam.

Moss is 22 percent overall and 30 percent in Korea. Half of all the Moss we
build in the quarter goes to two markets, and Moss has the longest coating lead
of the four.

## Carriers

Two carriers, A and B, and between them they have committed 71,000 units of the
180,000 in writing. That is just under 40 percent of the quarter sitting with
two partners.

| | A | B |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Markets | 4 | 4 |
| Committed units | 46,000 | 25,000 |

A covers Korea, the United States, Germany and Canada. B covers Japan, the
United Kingdom, Australia and France. The Netherlands has no carrier and sells
through open channel and direct only.

Neither carrier has an exclusive anywhere. Both schedules are drafts and neither
is countersigned. The target for signature is 30 September.

## What this means for each area

**Hardware.** Four finishes, not six. The finish mix is not the same in every
market and the two eastern markets pull hard on Moss.

**Firmware.** Nine markets in the first quarter means nine locales, and eight
of the nine were already in the shell. Tier 3 needs a right to left layout
across four panes that does not exist.

**Supply chain.** 71,000 units of the plan are somebody else's commitment with
somebody else's delivery conditions attached. One carrier will not accept a
finish split across two deliveries and the other will. Those conditions are not
interchangeable.

**Finance.** Every number on this page is a unit count. The money view is
John's and is built from these.

**Everyone.** Two carrier certifications sit in the same six week window as the
national approvals, and the Korean one has a single lab slot with nothing
behind it.
