# Packaging and accessories, a note from supply chain

Dana Provenzano
2026-09-06

Three people have asked me this week when packaging has to be at the factory,
and I have given three slightly different answers because I keep doing the
arithmetic in my head. This is the answer written down so that nobody has to
ask a fourth time and so that I stop being the single point of failure for a
date.

## The date

Packaging has to be at the Shenzhen line before the pilot run. Not the day of,
before. The line needs the sleeves, trays, wallets and bands physically on site
and counted, because a pilot run is partly a test of whether the packing station
works and you cannot test a packing station with a photograph of a box.

Working back from that, and this is the part people skip:

| Step | Duration | Who |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sea freight, printer to Shenzhen | Four weeks | Me |
| Print and finishing, sleeves | Two weeks | Printer, through Maya |
| Moulded fibre run, trays and accessory boxes | Three weeks, overlapping | Moulder, through me |
| Proof approval on final stock | One week | Maya |
| Art release | The gate | Priya |

The overlap is real and it is the only reason this works. The tray tooling is
already cut and the moulder can run trays while the sleeves are still being
proofed, because a tray has no printing on it. If the tray tooling had to change
again, that overlap disappears and the whole thing becomes serial.

Which is why I pushed back hard on the second tray revision and then agreed to
it anyway. Sam was right about the wedge. A tray that is an interference fit on
half the units is a tray that fails at the packing station in front of the
people we are trying to prove the line to.

Air freight exists and I do not want to use it. Fibre packaging is bulky and
light, which is the worst possible thing to put on a plane, and I would rather
spend the two weeks of slack we currently have than explain that decision later.

## What actually binds

The colourway names. Everything above waits on four words.

I have said this in three different rooms and I am going to say it once more in
writing. The names are printed on the sleeve. Four names, four sleeve variants,
one print run. If a name changes after art release, we do not adjust a file. We
throw away sleeves and buy a new slot on a press that is already busy, and the
slot after ours belongs to somebody else's Christmas.

Maya and Priya locked Ink, Chalk, Moss and Ember on the eighth. That is the gate
cleared and I want to thank whoever pushed it, because it was going to slip on
politeness rather than on any real disagreement.

## Accessories are a separate supply chain and people forget

The folio case and the desk stand do not come from the device factory. Different
vendors, different lead times, different boxes, different countries.

This matters for one reason. The device can be ready and the accessories can be
late, and if the accessories are late then the retail display units are
incomplete, because the counter fixture has a slot for a case and a stand next
to the phone.

Current position, and this is honest rather than comfortable:

The case is on vendor sample two after the first one would not fold properly.
Sam has the fix and the vendor accepted it without argument, which usually means
the change is easy for them. Sample two is due later this month. If sample two
is right, tooling for all four finishes runs off the same geometry and we are
fine. If sample two is wrong, the case is not at launch, and I would want to
know that in early October rather than in late October.

The stand is on printed prototypes. Production tooling is not cut. There is a
mechanical change in flight this week, a thicker boss around the magnet pocket
and a keyed pocket so the magnet cannot go in backwards. Sam sent me the section
view yesterday and flagged it as functional rather than cosmetic, which I
appreciated, because a thicker boss on a drawing looks exactly like the kind of
thing I would question on a cost review.

I am not cutting stand tooling until that change is in the drawing that the
vendor quotes from. Cutting tooling twice for a stand would be a genuinely
embarrassing way to spend a month.

## The thing I want to say to everyone

The packaging is a moulded fibre tray with no plastic in it. Not a bag around
the cable, not a window in the sleeve, not a clip holding the wallet.

That was decided when we were five people and it has survived every review
since. I have been asked twice to reconsider the cable bag and I have said no
twice, and I will keep saying no, because the alternative is that we make a
claim about the box and then somebody opens it and finds a small clear bag.

The paper band around the cable took three iterations to hold. It holds now.

## What I need

From Maya: confirmation on the day the sleeve files actually go, not the day
they are planned to go.

From Sam: the case sample two result the day it is on his bench, not the week
after.

From everybody: if you hear a colourway name being questioned in a room I am not
in, tell me the same day. That is the one change I cannot absorb.
