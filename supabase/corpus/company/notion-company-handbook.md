## What we are building

Supaphone makes the Fold S1, a four panel folding phone. Three hinges, four
panels, and it opens from a phone to a tablet to something the size of a desk
mat. There are seven of us. The device is pre production and headed for carrier
certification and a pilot line in Shenzhen.

That is the whole company in a paragraph. If a new person reads nothing else,
read that.

## The three words you will hear every day

| Word | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Meniscus | The outer display layer, the part you touch when the phone is shut |
| Bellows | The hinge assembly, all three hinges together |
| Ori | The shell and window manager, the software that decides what shows on how many panels |

The glossary page has the longer list. These three come up in almost every
conversation and using them wrong makes a meeting take twice as long.

## How we work

> Write the decision down before the meeting, in the page where the decision
> lives. A meeting that starts with somebody explaining the options out loud is
> a meeting that ends without a decision.

- Decisions live in Notion. Work lives in Linear. Conversation lives in Slack.
  A decision that only exists in Slack does not exist.
- When a decision reverses, the new one goes in the same page with a Supersedes
  line and the old one stays visible.
- Every page has one owner. Shared ownership means nobody updates it.
- Say the thing in the channel rather than in a direct message, unless it is
  about a person.

## What we do not do

- We do not run a change control board. There are seven of us.
- We do not have a formal on call rotation. Dev units are not customers.
- We do not build process for a company we are not yet.

**What if I think a process here is wrong?**
Change the page and say so in the channel. Every page in this space is editable
by everyone in it. Tell the owner what you changed.

**Where do I ask a question that does not fit a channel?**
General. Nobody minds.

## Working hours and where people are

We are spread across three time zones and we do not pretend otherwise. Overlap
is four hours in the middle of the European day. Anything that needs all seven
happens in that window. Everything else is written down and read later.
