## Touch a device

Everyone here holds a Fold S1 in their first week, whatever their job. You
cannot write about it, price it or plan a line for it without knowing what the
hinge feels like at 90 degrees.

1. Ask Sam or Dana for a carry unit.
2. Open it one panel at a time. Feel the two detents.
3. Put it through every Ori state: COVER, PHONE, BOOK, DESK, LAPTOP, TENT.
4. Shut it and look at the outer panel under a lamp at a shallow angle.
5. Give it back, or sign for it if it becomes yours.

> Carry units are engineering samples. They do not leave the building except
> with a signature, they do not get photographed, and they do not get shown to
> anyone outside the company. Sam's lab and sample handling page has the rules.

**Nobody has given me anything to do.**
Normal for about three days, and then it stops being normal. Say so.

**How much of the wiki should I read?**
The four pages above and then whatever your area table says. The rest of it is
reference material and reading it front to back is a way of avoiding work.
