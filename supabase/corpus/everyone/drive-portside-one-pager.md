Portside

One thread per shipment. Everything in it.

Every shipment gets a thread. The email from the carrier, the status
messages off the shipper's EDI feed, the rate confirmation, the bill of
lading, the four photos the driver took at the dock. All of it in one
place, in order, where the whole team can read it.

It stays out of one person's Outlook and out of the spreadsheet that
opens for one person at a time.

What it replaces

- The shared mailbox nobody trusts
- The spreadsheet with the load numbers in it
- The three people forwarding the same email to each other
- The phone call that starts "do you have the rate con on this one"

Who it is for

Freight brokerages and small logistics firms, roughly four to sixty
people. If your dispatchers work from a mailbox and your operations
manager keeps the truth in a spreadsheet, this is built for you.

What is in it

Threads Every shipment is one thread. Email, EDI, files, notes.
EDI in Status messages land in the thread as they arrive. 214 and 204.
Files Rate confirmations, bills of lading, PODs, photos, all attached.
Statuses Tender, dispatched, in transit, delivered, invoiced.
People Assign a thread. Hand it over at the end of a shift.
Search Find a thread by carrier, shipper, reference or word.
