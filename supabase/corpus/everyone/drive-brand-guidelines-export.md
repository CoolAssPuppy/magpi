The wordmark

The wordmark is Alderwick set in Marlin Grotesk Medium with the
tracking tightened to minus 12. It is not a font you can retype. Use
the supplied file.

There are three files and only three:

alderwick-wordmark-ink.svg On light backgrounds
alderwick-wordmark-paper.svg On dark or photographic backgrounds
alderwick-wordmark-mono.svg Single colour, for print and for fax

There is no icon. There is no monogram. People ask for an A in a square
about twice a year and the answer is still no.

Clear space

Minimum clear space on all four sides is equal to the height of the
lowercase letter d in the wordmark. Call it one d.

Nothing goes inside that box. Not a tagline, not a partner logo, not
the edge of a photograph, not a slide number.

Minimum sizes
Screen 96 px wide
Print 24 mm wide

Below those sizes the descender on the k fills in and it stops reading
as a word. If you need it smaller than that, you need a different
layout.

Colour

Ink #16211C Primary. Body text, wordmark, most things.
Paper #F7F5F0 Background. Warmer than white and it matters.
Rope #C4703A The accent. Buttons, links, one thing per screen.
Harbour #2F5D62 Secondary accent. Charts, diagrams, illustration.
Slate #6B7671 Muted text, borders, disabled states.
Signal #B23A2E Errors only. Never decorative.

Contrast: Ink on Paper, Paper on Ink, and Paper on Rope all pass at
normal body sizes. Rope on Paper does not pass for small text and must
not be used for it. Rope is a button colour, not a text colour.

Typography

Two typefaces. Only two.

Marlin Grotesk Headings, the wordmark, buttons, anything short.
Weights: Regular, Medium. Not Bold. We do not license Bold.

Coyle Text Body copy, long documents, tables, the product.
Weights: Regular, Italic, Semibold.

In the product, numbers in tables use Coyle Text with tabular figures
switched on, so columns line up. This is set in the stylesheet and you
should not have to think about it.

Fallback stack for email and anywhere the licence does not reach:
system sans for headings, system serif for body. Do not substitute a
lookalike.

What not to do with the wordmark

- Do not stretch it, condense it, or scale it non proportionally.
- Do not rotate it. It sits flat.
- Do not add a drop shadow, an outline, a gradient, or a bevel.
- Do not change its colour to anything outside the palette on page 4.
- Do not put it on a busy photograph without the paper version and
enough contrast to read.
- Do not set it in a different typeface and call it the wordmark.
- Do not add a registered mark. We do not use one.
- Do not lock it up with a product name. Portside has its own
treatment and it is on page 7.

Portside

Portside is the product. Alderwick is the company. They are set
differently on purpose.

Portside is set in Marlin Grotesk Regular, tracking normal, always in
Ink or Paper. Never in Rope.

Where both appear, the correct form is "Portside by Alderwick", with
the two words at the same size and the wordmark file used for
Alderwick. There is a supplied lockup for this. Use it rather than
building your own out of two files.

Tone of voice

We write like people who have done the job. Short sentences, plain
words, and the specific thing rather than the general one. We say rate
confirmation, not documentation. We say your dispatcher, not the
end user. We are allowed to be dry and we are not allowed to be
clever at a customer's expense. When something has gone wrong we say
what happened and what we are doing, in that order, without a
paragraph of apology in front of it. When we do not know, we say we do
not know and when we will. No exclamation marks in the product. One
per marketing page, if you have earned it, which you probably have
not.
