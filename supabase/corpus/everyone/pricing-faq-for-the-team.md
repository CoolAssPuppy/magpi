# Pricing: what to say and what not to say

Owner: Elena Vargas
Last edited: 24 August 2026
Audience: everyone at Alderwick

People keep asking me about pricing in the kitchen, in Slack, and once in the
car park. This page is so I can stop answering it one person at a time.

## Is pricing changing?

Yes. There is a pricing change coming and leadership is working on it now.

## What are the new prices?

Not agreed yet, and not mine to share when they are. Diane and Marcus are
finalising it. The numbers exist in a leadership document and that is where
they are staying until the announcement.

I know that is annoying. It is annoying for me too, because I am the one on
calls with customers who can read a renewal date on a calendar.

## When does it happen?

The change lands in the autumn. There will be an announcement to customers
before anything takes effect, and everyone here will hear about it before the
customers do. I am not going to be more specific than that on a page the whole
company can read, because a date that moves is worse than no date.

## What do I say if a customer asks?

Say that pricing is under review and that they will hear from us directly
before anything changes for them. Then send me the account name.

Do not quote a number. Do not confirm a direction. Do not say "it is going up"
even if you are confident it is going up, because a customer who hears that
from a support agent in August will treat it as a commitment in October and
they will be right to.

If they push, the honest answer is: we are reviewing pricing, nothing changes
on your account without notice from us, and I will have your account manager
call you.

## What about accounts renewing soon?

Send me the account name. There are a small number of accounts where the timing
is awkward and leadership is handling those individually. I know which ones.

## Is my own account manager quota changing?

No. Compensation and quota are a separate conversation and nothing there is
changing this quarter.

## Who do I ask?

Me first. If it is a specific account, me and only me. If it is a general
question about the change itself, Diane.

Please stop asking Ade. He does not know either and he has a month end to run.
